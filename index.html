About Me

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Northeastern University (Class of 2028), driven by curiosity about how things are made and a desire to be constantly improving and learning. Before pursuing engineering, I explored everything from building computers to game design in Unreal Engine to 3D modeling in Blender. A high school engineering class introduced me to CAD, microcontrollers, and physical systems, and I found that engineering brought together the creative and technical instincts I'd been developing for years.

My favorite projects are the ones that push me to work across disciplines: designing mechanical systems, writing embedded control logic, prototyping enclosures, and iterating until the whole system works together. Whether that means tuning gear ratios on a fuel cell car through systematic testing, designing a multi-stage puzzle box from scratch in under two weeks, or building an animatronic snapping turtle where Blender skills from game design became essential to the mechanical build, I want to understand and learn as much as I can.

Skills: SolidWorks, OnShape, Blender, Arduino/ESP32 (C++), 3D printing, CNC machining, rapid prototyping, hands-on fabrication and assembly, and more (see resume for full list).
