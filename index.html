About Me

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Northeastern University (Class of 2028), driven by curiosity about how things are made and a desire to be constantly improving and learning. Before finding engineering, I explored everything from building computers to game design in Unreal Engine to 3D modeling in Blender, and when a high school engineering class introduced me to CAD, microcontrollers, and physical systems, everything clicked. Engineering gave me a way to bring together the creative and technical instincts I'd been developing for years.

My favorite projects are the ones that push me to work across disciplines: designing mechanical systems, writing embedded control logic, prototyping enclosures, and iterating until the whole thing works together. Whether that means tuning gear ratios on a fuel cell car through systematic testing, designing a multi-stage puzzle box from scratch in under two weeks, or building an animatronic snapping turtle where Blender skills from game design became essential to the mechanical build, I want to understand the full picture.
